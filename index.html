How to play

Load sats.
Drop balls.

A digital pin game inspired by Bingolett – played in your browser and powered by Bitcoin Lightning.

A Works best with Alby GoAny Lightning wallet works · Alby Go offers direct opening on iOS and macOS ↗
  1. 01

    Deposit

    Press Insert Sats, choose an amount and pay the Lightning invoice with your wallet. The sats will then appear in your balance.

  2. 02

    Play

    One round costs 10 sats. Launch three balls, one after another. Each ball activates its landing number on the paytable.

  3. 03

    Win

    If all three fields in a column are active after the third ball, the prize shown below it is automatically added to your balance.

  4. 04

    Withdraw

    Press Payout to withdraw your available balance. Scan the LNURL QR code or use a Lightning invoice instead.
